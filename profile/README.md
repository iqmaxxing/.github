# IQMAXXING

Free games for working memory, processing speed, attention control and reasoning, in your browser at **[iqmaxxing.org](https://iqmaxxing.org)**. No account needed, no ads, and every run is saved and charted.

- [Dual n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/dual) and [quad n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/quad)
- [Single n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/single) and [3D cube n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/cube)
- [Digit span](https://iqmaxxing.org/digit), [Corsi blocks](https://iqmaxxing.org/corsi) and the [working memory tests](https://iqmaxxing.org/working-memory-test)
- [Stroop](https://iqmaxxing.org/stroop), [reaction time](https://iqmaxxing.org/rt) and [relational reasoning](https://iqmaxxing.org/reason)
- [How to read your n-back score](https://iqmaxxing.org/theory/reading-your-score) and [what IQ maxxing means](https://iqmaxxing.org/theory/iq-maxxing)

On iPhone and iPad: [IQMAXXING: Dual N-Back](https://apps.apple.com/app/iqmaxxing-dual-n-back/id6825253595) on the App Store.
