# IQMAXXING

IQMAXXING is a free, ad-free brain training site and app for iPhone, iPad and Android, with dual, quad and 3D n-back, relational reasoning (RRT) and CCT, where your runs are scored per stream and saved.

Play in your browser at **[iqmaxxing.org](https://iqmaxxing.org)**. You need no account.

- [Dual n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/dual)
- [Quad n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/quad), and on a turning cube as [3D quad n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/quad3d)
- [Relational reasoning (RRT)](https://iqmaxxing.org/reason)
- [CCT](https://iqmaxxing.org/pasat), serial addition at a pace that follows you
- [Single n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/single) and [3D cube n-back](https://iqmaxxing.org/cube)
- [Digit span](https://iqmaxxing.org/digit), [Corsi blocks](https://iqmaxxing.org/corsi), [Stroop](https://iqmaxxing.org/stroop) and the [working memory tests](https://iqmaxxing.org/working-memory-test)

The site has 34 games. The app has 24 of them and can share one history with the site.

- iPhone and iPad: [IQMAXXING: Dual N-Back](https://apps.apple.com/app/iqmaxxing-dual-n-back/id6825253595) on the App Store
- Android: [IQMAXXING: Dual N-Back Trainer](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=org.iqmaxxing.app) on Google Play
